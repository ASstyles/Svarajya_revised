## Planning Notes

- `Fully` means Codex can generate the artifact from local evidence, but it still requires human sign-off before being treated as final audit truth.
- `Partially` means Codex can produce an evidence-backed artifact, but live behavior, intent, ownership, or ambiguous mappings need PM/WD confirmation.
- `Draft only` means Codex can prepare a proposed artifact, but it should not be used for implementation, release, or final handover without human review.
- Any deliverable that depends on unavailable evidence must mark affected findings as `Unconfirmed` or `Needs WD Verification`.
- Existing files in `audit-input/02-existing-team-outputs/` may support these deliverables, but they are not automatically considered verified AI outputs.
